OUR STORY

A quiet retreat born from
a love for slow living.

AMARA was founded with a singular intention: to build an unhurried, sunlit sanctuary amidst the vibrant pulse of Bengaluru. A space where architecture, tropical foliage, and the scent of freshly ground beans come together.

Every cup begins with beans sourced from heritage hill estates in Southern India, roasted in small batches to preserve their delicate notes of jaggery, stone fruit, and dark cacao. Our bakery wakes up before dawn, laminating French butter croissants and pulling artisanal loaves straight from the oven.
